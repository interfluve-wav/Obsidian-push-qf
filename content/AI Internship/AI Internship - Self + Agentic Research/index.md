# AI Internship

> Neurology AI only. No gastro. No GI. This folder is the primary research artifact for the 8-week capstone.

---

## What this is

An 8-week AI internship capstone focused on **neurology** — specifically: ambient AI documentation in the clinical encounter, pre-visit synthesis from longitudinal data, and caregiver-in-the-loop tooling.

The goal is to produce a self-contained strategic document (problem statement, opportunity wedge, technical feasibility signals, dataset availability, action items) that lives here AND in the chat, ready to hand to a clinical collaborator or technical co-founder.

---

## Recommended subspecialty wedges

| Subspecialty | Why | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Headache / Migraine** | Highest outpatient volume; heavy caregiver burden; pre-visit chart review is painful | Good candidate for pre-visit synth + migraine diary integration |
| **Cognitive / Dementia** | Longitudinal by definition; caregiver-in-the-loop is essential, not optional; pre-visit synth is a direct win | Caregiver tools, progression tracking |
| **Epilepsy** | Seizure diaries, medication tracking, emergency context | Real-time + longitudinal fusion |

## Research tracks

### Track A — Market / Strategy *(Suhaas)*
- Competitors (Abridge, Viz.ai, Ceribell, NuDuo, etc.)
- Pricing, business models, regulatory status
- Pain points with quantified data
- Go-to-market options

### Track B — Clinical Domain *(Collaborator)*
- Subspecialty deep-dives
- Caregiver workflows
- Doctor interviews
- Unmet gap mapping

### Track C — Technical Feasibility *(Suhaas)*
- Dataset availability (MIMIC, eICU, CheXpert, NeuroGPT, etc.)
- Model architectures (ASR, LLM, fine-tuning)
- Evaluation metrics (WER, MTR, confabulation/hallucination guards)
- Technical risk signals

## Principles

- Every source claim: include verbatim quote + author/source + score/attribution where applicable
- Every number: pricing, accuracy %, sample size, FDA status — concrete, not vague
- Every competitor teardown: end with "what this means for us"
- Every planning doc: end with "what to do next" action items
- No gastro. No GI. If a reference touches GI, flag and exclude.
