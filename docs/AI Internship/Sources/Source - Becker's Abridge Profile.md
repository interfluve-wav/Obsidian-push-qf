## Summary

Becker's Health IT profile (March 2025, by Molly Gamble) of Abridge founder/CEO Dr. Shiv Rao — a practicing cardiologist — and the company's trajectory from "AI tutorials for executives" in 2018 to serving 100+ health systems in 2025. Covers the Series D ($250M, $2.75B valuation), leadership team, nurse-focused AI expansion, customer outcomes at Inova, Duke Health, Endeavor Health, and the company's culture of "eating glass."

## Key Claims

### Company Scale
- 100+ health systems (up from fewer systems a few years prior)
- 1,000+ physicians at Inova (Virginia and Washington D.C.)
- 5,000 clinicians at Duke Health
- 28+ languages, 50+ specialties
- $250M Series D (2025), led by Elad Gil + IVP
- Valuation: $2.75B

### Leadership Team
- **Shiv Rao, MD** — Founder and CEO; practicing cardiologist; previously led provider-facing investment portfolio at UPMC
- **Zachary C. Lipton, PhD** — Chief Technology and Science Officer; associate professor at Carnegie Mellon University
- **Julia Chou** — COO (former Google)
- **Mario Queiroz** — Chief Product Officer (former Google)
- **Tim Hwang** — General Counsel (former Google)
- **Sagar Sanghvi** — CFO (former CFO of Instacart)
- **Brian Wilson** — Chief Commercial Officer (healthcare veteran)

### Customer Outcomes
- **Inova Health System:** "no shift in work, just eliminated keystrokes"; pajama time as key KPI; more notes closed same day; patient visits trending upward; 1,000+ physicians using Abridge
- **Duke Health:** 5,000 clinicians expanded; Dr. Eric Poon (Chief Health Information Officer) — left clinic with documentation complete for first time in years
- **Endeavor Health:** pilot scaled in 3 months (planned for 6); 100% increase in clinicians' ability to give patients undivided attention; 60%+ improved work satisfaction; described as "phenomenal" by VP of Innovation
- **Memorial Sloan Kettering, Mayo Clinic, Johns Hopkins Medicine, Duke Health, UNC Health, Christus Health, UChicago Medicine, Endeavor Health, Akron Children's** — named as customer health systems

### Nurse Expansion
- Working with Mayo Clinic + Epic on nurse-specific generative AI ambient documentation (since summer 2024)
- Nurses use discrete forms/cells/flow sheets (vs. physician narrative style) — requires distinct product
- Mayo Chief Nursing Officer Ryannon Frederick: "It's not Abridge building a tool that Mayo then tests. It's Mayo nurses working side by side with Abridge and Epic"
- Potential wedge into nursing workflow documentation — distinct from physician scribes

### Culture
- "Hope camels": Abridge early team metaphor for surviving on optimism through challenges
- "Eating glass": Abridge's internal metaphor for embracing difficulty (inspired by Jensen Huang's Stanford talk)
- CEO still takes weekend hospital shifts monthly — keeps him grounded as an end user

### Strategic Framing
- "AI is just an incredible tool, and what we're building right now is really the most initial wedge into a much larger opportunity to improve experiences, to improve outcomes and to lower costs over time" — Dr. Shiv Rao
- Focus on "demanding pilots, high standards, and candid feedback" — competitive differentiation through rigor
- Target: complex AND large/academic health systems (not just one or the other)

## Contradictions / Tensions

- **Valuation vs. revenue:** $2.75B valuation stated; no public revenue figures disclosed. At $89.1M (Ceribell FY25 revenue), Abridge's $2.75B valuation implies a much higher revenue multiple — requires validation from public financials or KLAS data.
- **"Eating glass" culture:** while framed positively, the Becker's piece is PR — actual employee experience (Glassdoor, Blind) would provide a fuller picture.
