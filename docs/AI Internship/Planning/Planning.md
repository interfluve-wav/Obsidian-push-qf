This approach successfully automated pre-work for radiologists by providing preliminary analysis and recommendations ([00:09:54](https://docs.google.com/document/d/15wIwD0iglUGIgQAfPbQVW9r3TbTjLMeMCkanYeJn3vY/edit?tab=t.8sm4aoktyro4#heading=h.hf00c2gav27h)).
