# Endnotes & Citations

---

[^1]: Standard clinical note formats. SOAP = Subjective, Objective, Assessment, Plan. The most common structure for physician documentation in the US.

[^2]: An open interoperability standard that lets third-party healthcare apps read from EHRs. Abridge uses SMART on FHIR to write notes back into Epic.

[^3]: Per FierceHealthcare (Jun 2025): "The company has raised approximately $800 million." Series E at $5.3B valuation, Feb 2025.

[^4]: A peer-reviewed, open-access medical journal published by the American Medical Association. JAMIA Open is indexed in PubMed and considered high-quality for health informatics research.

[^5]: KLAS Research is an independent healthcare IT rating firm. Their reports are widely cited in health tech and represent provider-voted assessments of vendor performance.

[^6]: Abridge supports 28 languages including English, Spanish, Mandarin (Cantonese), Japanese, Arabic, Hindi, Portuguese, French, German, and others.

[^7]: The Mini-Z is a single-question validated physician burnout assessment tool. Developed by the AMA and widely used in burnout research.

[^8]: The 81%, 77%, 73%, 67%, 64% figures come from the post-implementation survey of Abridge users across multiple health systems, cited in Abridge's own published materials.

[^9]: The University of Kansas Medical Center, based in Kansas City, KS. One of the leading academic medical centers studying ambient AI documentation.

[^10]: The standard metric for ASR accuracy. The percentage of words incorrectly transcribed. Lower is better. Medical ASR WER benchmarks are typically 15–25% depending on specialty and audio quality.

[^11]: The technology that converts spoken audio into written text. Medical ASR is field-specific ASR trained on clinical conversations, medical terminology, and multi-speaker audio.

[^12]: The dominant US hospital EHR system (~30–40% hospital market share). Abridge's Epic integration is its deepest and most strategic partnership.

[^13]: Confirmed in the Tierney et al. KUMC paper: "median draft note generation time decreased from 76 seconds (mid-2023) to 38 seconds (mid-2024)."

[^14]: Abridge is regulated as clinical documentation software, not a medical device. This sidesteps FDA 510(k) clearance requirements.

[^15]: The gold-standard clinical assessment for Parkinson's disease severity. Covers motor and non-motor symptoms across the disease spectrum.

[^16]: The gold-standard disability measurement for multiple sclerosis (MS), scored 0-10 in half-point increments. EDSS measures functional system scores and mobility.

[^17]: A patient-reported questionnaire scoring migraine disability over 3 months. MIDAS grades: 0-5 (little or no disability), 6-10 (mild), 11-20 (moderate), 21+ (severe).

[^18]: A text shortcut in an EHR that expands into a longer pre-formatted clinical text block. Used for standard procedures, routine orders, and commonly documented items.
