> AI Internship · Neurology · Competitor Research

---

## What Abridge Actually Is

Abridge sits in the room with the doctor and patient, records the conversation, transcribes it in real time, and drafts a clinical note. The doctor reviews and edits the note before it goes into the EHR. The patient never sees it.

The doctor opens the app on their phone, the patient consents verbally, and Abridge handles the rest — ambient capture, AI draft, physician sign-off, EHR write-back.
